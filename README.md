# Okta Operating Model Transformation

*Independent consulting case study using financial and operating data from Okta, Inc.'s FY2026 Form 10-K (SEC EDGAR). This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Okta, Inc.*

### Strategy · Operating Model Design · Business Transformation

> **Business question:** How can Okta maintain operating efficiency as its workforce becomes more global and service delivery shifts toward external partners?

---

## Case Overview

From FY2024 to FY2026, Okta reduced operating expenses from **97% to 72% of revenue** and improved its operating margin from **-22.8% to 5.1%**.

At the same time, headcount growth resumed, international employees increased as a share of the workforce, and Okta began shifting more professional services delivery to external partners. This case evaluates the operating changes needed to manage that growth while maintaining the efficiency gains made over the past three years.

---

## Key Findings

- Headcount increased **7.6% in FY2026** after two years of limited growth.
- International employees increased from **33% to 44%** of the workforce between FY2024 and FY2026.
- Customers with more than **$100K in ACV increased to 5,100**.
- More professional services engagements are expected to shift to partners beginning in FY2027, increasing the need for clear ownership and performance oversight.

---

## Recommendations

### 01 | Clarify Governance and Decision Rights

Define ownership for key decisions, reduce unnecessary approval layers, and establish consistent accountability across regions.

### 02 | Improve Cross-Functional Execution

Clarify handoffs across Sales, Customer Success, Product, and Operations following Okta's sales organization changes.

### 03 | Build Controls for Partner Delivery

Set clear ownership, service expectations, and performance measures for engagements delivered by external partners.

---

## Implementation

**Assess → Design → Pilot → Scale**

| Phase | Timeline | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Assess** | 0–30 Days | Validate ownership, decision rights, process issues, and baseline performance |
| **Design** | 30–90 Days | Define the operating model, governance structure, and accountability framework |
| **Pilot** | 3–6 Months | Test selected process and governance changes |
| **Scale** | 6–12 Months | Expand successful changes and establish ongoing performance monitoring |

---

## Value at Stake

Based on Okta's FY2026 revenue of **$2.92B**, each one percentage-point reduction in operating expenses as a share of revenue represents approximately **$29M in annual operating expense impact**.

This is a sensitivity analysis based on FY2026 revenue, not an estimate of savings attributable to the recommendations.
