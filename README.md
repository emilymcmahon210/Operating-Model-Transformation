# Operating Model Transformation | Okta

### Scaling for Profitable Growth

> **Independent consulting case study examining how a global technology company can evolve its operating model to sustain efficiency, strengthen execution, and support continued growth.**

---

## Executive Summary

Okta has demonstrated significant improvements in operating performance while continuing to grow its customer base and global footprint. From FY2024 to FY2026, revenue increased from approximately $2.3B to $2.9B while operating expenses declined as a percentage of revenue and the company transitioned from an operating loss to profitability.

As Okta continues to scale, increasing organizational complexity, enterprise customer growth, global expansion, and greater use of its partner ecosystem create new operating model considerations.

This case evaluates how Okta can build on recent efficiency gains while creating an operating model capable of supporting its next phase of profitable growth.

---

## The Business Challenge

### How can Okta evolve its operating model to sustain efficiency gains while enabling scalable, profitable growth?

The analysis focuses on four dimensions of the operating model:

- **People** — Organizational capacity, accountability, and workforce scalability
- **Process** — Standardization and cross-functional execution
- **Governance** — Decision rights, ownership, and performance management
- **Technology & Information** — Visibility, reporting, and decision support

## Key Business Insights

|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue | $2.26B | $2.61B | $2.92B |
| Operating Margin | -22.8% | -2.8% | 5.1% |

| Employees | 5,908 | 5,914 | 6,366 |
| Revenue per Employee | $383K | $441K | $459K |

### What the data suggests

Okta has demonstrated meaningful operating leverage. Revenue increased substantially between FY2024 and FY2026 while operating expenses grew at a significantly slower rate, contributing to a transition from operating losses to profitability.

At the same time, continued organizational growth introduces new considerations around governance, coordination, and scalability.

---

# Transformation Recommendations

## 01 | Simplify Governance

Clarify decision rights, ownership, and accountability across increasingly global and specialized functions.

**Focus areas**
- Define clear decision ownership
- Reduce unnecessary approval complexity
- Establish enterprise-wide governance standards
- Create consistent performance accountability

---

## 02 | Strengthen Cross-Functional Execution

Create stronger coordination mechanisms across Sales, Customer Success, Product, and Operations to support increasingly complex enterprise customers.

**Focus areas**
- Establish clear cross-functional ownership
- Standardize critical handoffs
- Align functional KPIs with enterprise objectives
- Improve visibility into execution and performance

---

## 03 | Scale Through the Partner Ecosystem

Develop a structured operating model for activities increasingly delivered through external partners.

**Focus areas**
- Define partner governance and ownership
- Establish service-level expectations
- Implement partner performance measures
- Maintain consistent customer experience across delivery channels

---

# Transformation Roadmap

| Phase | Timeline | Primary Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Assess** | 0–30 Days | Validate process ownership, decision rights, pain points, and baseline performance |
| **Design** | 30–90 Days | Develop target operating model, governance structure, and accountability framework |
| **Pilot** | 3–6 Months | Test redesigned processes and governance within selected functions |
| **Scale** | 6–12 Months | Expand successful changes and establish continuous performance monitoring |

## Measuring Success

A transformation should be evaluated across both financial and operational performance.

**Financial**
- Operating margin
- Operating expense as % of revenue
- Revenue per employee

**Operational**
- Process cycle time
- Decision cycle time
- Cross-functional delivery performance

**Customer**
- Dollar-based net retention
- Enterprise customer growth
- Service delivery performance

**Organization**
- Span of control
- Role clarity
- Accountability and decision ownership

**Partner Ecosystem**
- Partner-delivered engagements
- SLA performance
- Customer outcomes
